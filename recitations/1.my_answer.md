## Formal / English Statements

1. There are exactly 3 people in the cabal.
2. At least one of Stav or David is in the cabal.
3. If Martyna or Patrice is in the cabal, then everyone is in the cabal.
4. If Stav is in the cabal, then David is also in the cabal.
5. If Darren is in the cabal, then Martyna is also in the cabal.
6. If Oscar or Nick is in the cabal, then Tom is not in the cabal.
7. If Oscar or David is in the cabal, then Marten is not in the cabal.

---

## Step-by-Step Solution Flow

### 1. Total Count Constraint (Rule 1)

**Analysis:** There are exactly 3 members in the cabal out of 9 total course staff members. Therefore, not everyone can be in the cabal.

### 2. Defer Rule 2

**Analysis:** Rule 2 states at least one of Stav or David is in the cabal. We do not have enough information to resolve this yet, so we revisit it after evaluating the conditional rules.

### 3. Eliminate Martyna and Patrice (Rule 3)

**Analysis:** Rule 3 states that if either Martyna or Patrice were in the cabal, *everyone* would be in the cabal. Since only 3 people can be in the cabal (Rule 1), neither can be a member.

**Conclusion:** Neither **Martyna** nor **Patrice** is in the cabal.

### 4. Determine Stav and David's Status (Rules 2 & 4)

**Analysis:**
* Rule 4 states: $\text{Stav} \implies \text{David}$.
* Rule 2 states: $\text{Stav} \lor \text{David}$.
* If Stav were in the cabal, David would also have to be in it. However, if Stav is NOT in the cabal, David MUST be in it to satisfy Rule 2. Thus, David is in the cabal regardless.
* Since we need room for other required members across the remaining rules, Stav must be out.


**Conclusion:** **David** is in the cabal, and **Stav** is not.

### 5. Eliminate Darren (Rule 5)

**Analysis:** Rule 5 states: $\text{Darren} \implies \text{Martyna}$. Since Martyna is not in the cabal (Step 3), Darren cannot be in the cabal either by *modus tollens*.

**Conclusion:** **Darren** is not in the cabal.

### 6. analysis of 6 statement

**Analysis:** we don't have any infromation about three of them yet. but if Tom is in cabal, both Oscar and Nick will not be in cabel (modus tollens)


### 7. Eliminate Marten (Rule 7)

**Analysis:** Rule 7 states: $(\text{Oscar} \lor \text{David}) \implies \neg\text{Marten}$. Since David is in the cabal (Step 4), Marten must be out.

**Conclusion:** **Marten** is not in the cabal.

### 8. Determine Remaining Spots: Oscar, Nick, and Tom (Rule 6)

**Current Roster Status:**
* **In (1/3):** David
* **Out (5):** Martyna, Patrice, Darren, Stav, Marten
* **Remaining Candidates (3):** Oscar, Nick, Tom (2 spots left to fill)


**Analysis:**
* Rule 6 states: $(\text{Oscar} \lor \text{Nick}) \implies \neg\text{Tom}$.
* If **Tom** were in the cabal, both Oscar and Nick would be excluded by *modus tollens*. That would leave only 2 total members in the cabal (David and Tom), violating Rule 1.
* Picking only one of Oscar or Nick would also fail to fill the required 3 spots without introducing Tom.


**Conclusion:** **Tom** is not in the cabal, which leaves **Oscar** and **Nick** to fill the remaining two spots.

---

## Final Roster

The 3 members of the cabal are **David**, **Oscar**, and **Nick**.
